How long is the Pre-Licensing course?+
The NC Pre-Licensing course is 75 hours total — the full state-required curriculum. Sessions are structured to fit your schedule, with options on weekdays and weekends.
Do I need prior experience to enroll?+
No prior real estate experience is required. The Pre-Licensing course is designed for complete beginners and covers everything you need to pass the NC licensing exam and launch your career.
Is W.I.S.E. approved by the NC Real Estate Commission?+
Yes. W.I.S.E. is an NCREC-approved education provider, Provider #1831. All courses count toward your NC licensing requirements and continuing education obligations.
What's included in the $499 Pre-Licensing tuition?+
All course materials and exam preparation are included in the flat $499 tuition. There are no hidden fees or required add-ons — one price covers everything you need to be ready for your exam.
Can I complete my continuing education at W.I.S.E.?+
Yes. The General Update CE course satisfies the annual continuing education requirement for NC license renewal. It's 4 hours, fully NCREC compliant, and priced at $49.
How do I reserve a seat in an upcoming class?+
Browse upcoming class dates in the Schedule section above and click "Book Now" or "Enroll" to secure your spot. You can also use the Enroll Now button at the top of the page.
